Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Steel, Pipe and Flange Pro ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, login, or any form of user registration.

The only data stored by the App is a single preference flag (whether you have acknowledged the in-app disclaimer). This flag is stored exclusively on your device using the device's secure local storage and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

2. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer any data to third parties. There are no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries integrated into the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not connect to any external servers or APIs. All dimensional data is bundled locally within the App and does not require an internet connection to function.

4. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for professional and educational use by adults. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not collect any information from children under 13.

5. Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of personal data exposure through use of this App. Your device's built-in security mechanisms protect any locally stored preferences.
