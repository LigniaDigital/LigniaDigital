Site Visit

Last updated: 11 August, 2026

Site Visit is an offline-first app for recording site visits, inspections, and maintenance checks. Organize your notes under simple headings, each with photos, text notes, and voice notes, then export everything into a single report.

How it works

Create a project for each site visit. Within a project, add headings for each area, item, or topic you need to document. Under each heading you can:

  • Take or attach photos
  • Write text notes
  • Record voice notes

Exporting

When you're done, export the project. Site Visit generates a PDF report containing all your headings, photos, and text notes, and packages it together with your voice note recordings into a single ZIP file. You can share the export or save it directly to your phone's storage.

Works offline

Site Visit does not require an internet connection to use. All your projects, photos, and voice notes are stored locally on your device.

Pricing

Site Visit is free to use, including three free exports. After that, purchase additional exports as needed:

  • 1 Export for $0.99
  • 10 Exports for $6.99
  • Unlimited Exports for $20

No subscriptions, no ads.

Privacy

Site Visit does not collect any user data. Everything you record stays on your device unless you choose to share or save an export yourself.
